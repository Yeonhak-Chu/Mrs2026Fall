Phase-Field Simulation of Sintering and DIGM in Gd2O3-ZrO2 Ceramic Composites

Yeonhak Chu, Yanzhou Ji, Sang-chae Jeon, Kunok Chang

Supplementary derivation for the poster. (아래는 형식 예시입니다. 실제 유도 과정으로 바꿔 넣으세요.)

1. Governing equation

For a concentration-dependent diffusivity $D(c)$ in one dimension,

\begin{equation} \frac{\partial c}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left( D(c)\,\frac{\partial c}{\partial x} \right). \label{eq:fick} \end{equation}

2. Boltzmann transformation

With $\eta = x/\sqrt{t}$ and $c(x,t) = c(\eta)$, the two sides of Eq. \eqref{eq:fick} become

\begin{align} \frac{\partial c}{\partial t} &= -\frac{\eta}{2t}\,\frac{\dd c}{\dd \eta}, \label{eq:lhs}\\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left( D\,\frac{\partial c}{\partial x} \right) &= \frac{1}{t}\,\frac{\dd}{\dd \eta}\left( D\,\frac{\dd c}{\dd \eta} \right). \label{eq:rhs} \end{align}

Result

Equating \eqref{eq:lhs} and \eqref{eq:rhs} gives the ordinary differential equation

\begin{equation} -\frac{\eta}{2}\,\frac{\dd c}{\dd \eta} = \frac{\dd}{\dd \eta}\left( D(c)\,\frac{\dd c}{\dd \eta} \right). \label{eq:ode} \end{equation}
