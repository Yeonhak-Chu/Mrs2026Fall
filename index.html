Phase-Field Simulation of Sintering and DIGM in Gd2O3-ZrO2 Ceramic Composites

Yeonhak Chu, Yanzhou Ji, Sang-chae Jeon, Kunok Chang

A. Eq. (2) from its definition · B. Why $\Delta G_\mathrm{int}\gt0$

A. Derivation of Eq. (2) from its definition

Notation. Grain $i$ is rotated by $\theta_i$ about $[001]\parallel z$ (crystal $\to$ lab):

\[ \mathbf a(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta&0\\ \sin\theta&\cos\theta&0\\ 0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad C_{ijkl}(\theta)=a_{ip}\,a_{jq}\,a_{kr}\,a_{ls}\,C_{pqrs}(0),\qquad \mathbf a(0)=\mathbf I . \]
\[ \begin{gathered} \mathbf a(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta&0\\ \sin\theta&\cos\theta&0\\ 0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\\ C_{ijkl}(\theta)=a_{ip}\,a_{jq}\,a_{kr}\,a_{ls}\,C_{pqrs}(0),\qquad \mathbf a(0)=\mathbf I . \end{gathered} \]

$\boldsymbol\varepsilon\equiv\boldsymbol\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}-\boldsymbol\varepsilon^0$ is the elastic strain, with the eigenstrain $\boldsymbol\varepsilon^0=\varepsilon^0(c)\,\mathbf I$ and the total strain $\boldsymbol\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}=\tfrac12\left(\nabla\mathbf u+\nabla\mathbf u^{\mathsf T}\right)$; $f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta;\boldsymbol\varepsilon)=\tfrac12\,\boldsymbol\varepsilon:\mathbf C(\theta):\boldsymbol\varepsilon$. $\Delta\varepsilon\equiv\varepsilon_{xx}-\varepsilon_{yy}=\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}_{xx}-\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}_{yy}$, $\gamma\equiv2\varepsilon_{xy}=2\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}_{xy}$ (the same with either strain, since $\boldsymbol\varepsilon^0$ is isotropic), $H\equiv C_{11}-C_{12}-2C_{44}$, $\theta_\Delta\equiv\theta_2-\theta_1$, $\theta_\Sigma\equiv\theta_1+\theta_2$.

\[ \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}\equiv f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta_1;\boldsymbol\varepsilon)-f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta_2;\boldsymbol\varepsilon)=\tfrac12\,\boldsymbol\varepsilon:\left[\mathbf C(\theta_1)-\mathbf C(\theta_2)\right]:\boldsymbol\varepsilon \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}&\equiv f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta_1;\boldsymbol\varepsilon)-f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta_2;\boldsymbol\varepsilon)\\ &=\tfrac12\,\boldsymbol\varepsilon:\left[\mathbf C(\theta_1)-\mathbf C(\theta_2)\right]:\boldsymbol\varepsilon \end{aligned} \]
(D0)

1. Rotated stiffness

\[ \begin{aligned} C_{ijkl}(0)&=C_{12}\,\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+C_{44}\left(\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}\right)+H\sum_{m=1}^{3}\delta_{im}\delta_{jm}\delta_{km}\delta_{lm},\\ C_{ijkl}(\theta)&=C_{12}\,\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+C_{44}\left(\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}\right)+H\sum_{m=1}^{3}a_{im}\,a_{jm}\,a_{km}\,a_{lm}. \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C_{ijkl}(0)&=C_{12}\,\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+C_{44}\left(\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}\right)\\ &\quad+H\sum_{m=1}^{3}\delta_{im}\delta_{jm}\delta_{km}\delta_{lm},\\ C_{ijkl}(\theta)&=C_{12}\,\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}+C_{44}\left(\delta_{ik}\delta_{jl}+\delta_{il}\delta_{jk}\right)\\ &\quad+H\sum_{m=1}^{3}a_{im}\,a_{jm}\,a_{km}\,a_{lm}. \end{aligned} \]
(D1)
\[ \begin{aligned} &\sum_m a_{1m}^4=\cos^4\theta+\sin^4\theta=1-\tfrac12\sin^22\theta,\qquad \sum_m a_{1m}^2a_{2m}^2=\tfrac12\sin^22\theta,\\ &\sum_m a_{1m}^3a_{2m}=-\sum_m a_{1m}a_{2m}^3=\tfrac14\sin4\theta \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} &\sum_m a_{1m}^4=\cos^4\theta+\sin^4\theta=1-\tfrac12\sin^22\theta,\\ &\sum_m a_{1m}^2a_{2m}^2=\tfrac12\sin^22\theta,\\ &\sum_m a_{1m}^3a_{2m}=-\sum_m a_{1m}a_{2m}^3=\tfrac14\sin4\theta \end{aligned} \]
(D2)
\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}(\theta)&=C_{2222}(\theta)=C_{11}-\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta, & C_{1122}(\theta)&=C_{12}+\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta,\\ C_{1212}(\theta)&=C_{44}+\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta, & C_{1112}(\theta)&=-C_{2212}(\theta)=\tfrac H4\sin4\theta . \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}(\theta)&=C_{2222}(\theta)=C_{11}-\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta,\\ C_{1122}(\theta)&=C_{12}+\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta,\\ C_{1212}(\theta)&=C_{44}+\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta,\\ C_{1112}(\theta)&=-C_{2212}(\theta)=\tfrac H4\sin4\theta . \end{aligned} \]
(D3)

Since $a_{3m}=\delta_{3m}$, every component with an index 3 is independent of $\theta$.

2. Energy of one grain

\[ \begin{aligned} f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta)={}&\tfrac12\Big[C_{1111}\left(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\right)+2C_{1122}\,\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}+C_{1212}\,\gamma^2\Big]\\ &+C_{1112}\,\gamma\left(\varepsilon_{xx}-\varepsilon_{yy}\right)+\left(\theta\text{-independent terms}\right) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta)={}&\tfrac12\Big[C_{1111}\left(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\right)\\ &\quad+2C_{1122}\,\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}+C_{1212}\,\gamma^2\Big]\\ &+C_{1112}\,\gamma\left(\varepsilon_{xx}-\varepsilon_{yy}\right)\\ &+\left(\theta\text{-independent terms}\right) \end{aligned} \]
(D4)

Inserting (D3):

\[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta\left[-\left(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\right)+2\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}+\gamma^2\right]+\tfrac H4\sin4\theta\,\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\\ &\quad=\tfrac H4\sin^22\theta\left(\gamma^2-\Delta\varepsilon^2\right)+\tfrac H4\sin4\theta\,\gamma\Delta\varepsilon , \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac H2\sin^22\theta\left[-\left(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\right)+2\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}+\gamma^2\right]\\ &\quad+\tfrac H4\sin4\theta\,\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\\ &=\tfrac H4\sin^22\theta\left(\gamma^2-\Delta\varepsilon^2\right)+\tfrac H4\sin4\theta\,\gamma\Delta\varepsilon , \end{aligned} \]
\[ f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta)=F+\frac H4\Big[\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\,\sin4\theta-\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\sin^22\theta\Big],\qquad F\ \text{independent of}\ \theta . \]
\[ \begin{aligned} f_{\mathrm{el}}(\theta)={}&F+\frac H4\Big[\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\,\sin4\theta\\ &-\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\sin^22\theta\Big],\\ &F\ \text{independent of}\ \theta . \end{aligned} \]
(D5)

3. Difference

\[ \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}=\frac H4\Big[\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\left(\sin4\theta_1-\sin4\theta_2\right)-\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\left(\sin^22\theta_1-\sin^22\theta_2\right)\Big] \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}={}&\frac H4\Big[\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\left(\sin4\theta_1-\sin4\theta_2\right)\\ &-\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\left(\sin^22\theta_1-\sin^22\theta_2\right)\Big] \end{aligned} \]
(D6)
\[ \begin{aligned} \sin4\theta_1-\sin4\theta_2&=2\cos(2\theta_1+2\theta_2)\,\sin(2\theta_1-2\theta_2)=-2\cos2\theta_\Sigma\,\sin2\theta_\Delta,\\ \sin^22\theta_1-\sin^22\theta_2&=\sin(2\theta_1+2\theta_2)\,\sin(2\theta_1-2\theta_2)=-\sin2\theta_\Sigma\,\sin2\theta_\Delta \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \sin4\theta_1-\sin4\theta_2&=2\cos(2\theta_1+2\theta_2)\,\sin(2\theta_1-2\theta_2)\\ &=-2\cos2\theta_\Sigma\,\sin2\theta_\Delta,\\ \sin^22\theta_1-\sin^22\theta_2&=\sin(2\theta_1+2\theta_2)\,\sin(2\theta_1-2\theta_2)\\ &=-\sin2\theta_\Sigma\,\sin2\theta_\Delta \end{aligned} \]
(D7)
\[ \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}=\frac H4\sin2\theta_\Delta\Big[\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Sigma-2\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\cos2\theta_\Sigma\Big] \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}={}&\frac H4\sin2\theta_\Delta\Big[\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Sigma\\ &-2\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\cos2\theta_\Sigma\Big] \end{aligned} \]
(D8)

4. $\beta$-rotated frame

\[ \beta\equiv\frac{\theta_\Sigma}{2}-\frac{\pi}{8} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4\beta=2\theta_\Sigma-\frac{\pi}{2},\qquad \sin2\theta_\Sigma=\cos4\beta,\qquad -\cos2\theta_\Sigma=\sin4\beta \]
\[ \begin{aligned} &\beta\equiv\frac{\theta_\Sigma}{2}-\frac{\pi}{8} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 4\beta=2\theta_\Sigma-\frac{\pi}{2},\\ &\sin2\theta_\Sigma=\cos4\beta,\qquad -\cos2\theta_\Sigma=\sin4\beta \end{aligned} \]
(D9)
\[ \Delta\varepsilon'\equiv\Delta\varepsilon\cos2\beta+\gamma\sin2\beta,\qquad \gamma'\equiv\gamma\cos2\beta-\Delta\varepsilon\sin2\beta \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta\varepsilon'&\equiv\Delta\varepsilon\cos2\beta+\gamma\sin2\beta,\\ \gamma'&\equiv\gamma\cos2\beta-\Delta\varepsilon\sin2\beta \end{aligned} \]
(D10)
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta\varepsilon'^2-\gamma'^2&=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\left(\cos^22\beta-\sin^22\beta\right)+4\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\sin2\beta\cos2\beta\\ &=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\cos4\beta+2\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\sin4\beta =\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Sigma-2\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\cos2\theta_\Sigma \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta\varepsilon'^2-\gamma'^2&=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\left(\cos^22\beta-\sin^22\beta\right)\\ &\quad+4\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\sin2\beta\cos2\beta\\ &=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\cos4\beta+2\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\sin4\beta\\ &=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2-\gamma^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Sigma-2\gamma\Delta\varepsilon\cos2\theta_\Sigma \end{aligned} \]
(D11)
\[ \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}=\frac H4\sin2\theta_\Delta\left(\Delta\varepsilon'^2-\gamma'^2\right) \]
(D12)

5. Complex form

\[ \zeta\equiv\Delta\varepsilon+i\gamma=\rho\,e^{i2\alpha},\qquad \rho^2=\Delta\varepsilon^2+\gamma^2 \]
(D13)
\[ \zeta\,e^{-i2\beta}=\left(\Delta\varepsilon+i\gamma\right)\left(\cos2\beta-i\sin2\beta\right)=\Delta\varepsilon'+i\gamma'=\rho\,e^{i\psi},\qquad \psi\equiv2\alpha-2\beta \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \zeta\,e^{-i2\beta}&=\left(\Delta\varepsilon+i\gamma\right)\left(\cos2\beta-i\sin2\beta\right)\\ &=\Delta\varepsilon'+i\gamma'=\rho\,e^{i\psi},\qquad \psi\equiv2\alpha-2\beta \end{aligned} \]
(D14)
\[ \begin{aligned} &\Delta\varepsilon'=\rho\cos\psi,\qquad \gamma'=\rho\sin\psi,\\ &\Delta\varepsilon'^2-\gamma'^2=\rho^2\left(\cos^2\psi-\sin^2\psi\right)=\rho^2\cos2\psi=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2+\gamma^2\right)\cos\left(4\alpha-4\beta\right) \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta\varepsilon'&=\rho\cos\psi,\qquad \gamma'=\rho\sin\psi,\\ \Delta\varepsilon'^2-\gamma'^2&=\rho^2\left(\cos^2\psi-\sin^2\psi\right)=\rho^2\cos2\psi\\ &=\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2+\gamma^2\right)\cos\left(4\alpha-4\beta\right) \end{aligned} \]
(D15)

6. Substituting $\beta$ and $\alpha$

\[ \cos\left(4\alpha-4\beta\right)=\cos\!\left(4\alpha-2\theta_\Sigma+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=-\sin\left(4\alpha-2\theta_\Sigma\right)=\sin\left(2\theta_\Sigma-4\alpha\right) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \cos\left(4\alpha-4\beta\right)&=\cos\!\left(4\alpha-2\theta_\Sigma+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\\ &=-\sin\left(4\alpha-2\theta_\Sigma\right)=\sin\left(2\theta_\Sigma-4\alpha\right) \end{aligned} \]
(D16)
\[ 2\alpha=\arctan\frac{\gamma}{\Delta\varepsilon}+k\pi\ \ (k\in\mathbb Z,\ \Delta\varepsilon\neq0) \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \sin\left(2\theta_\Sigma-4\alpha\right)=\sin2\!\left(\theta_\Sigma-\arctan\frac{\gamma}{\Delta\varepsilon}\right) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} &2\alpha=\arctan\frac{\gamma}{\Delta\varepsilon}+k\pi\ \ (k\in\mathbb Z,\ \Delta\varepsilon\neq0)\\ &\Longrightarrow\quad \sin\left(2\theta_\Sigma-4\alpha\right)=\sin2\!\left(\theta_\Sigma-\arctan\frac{\gamma}{\Delta\varepsilon}\right) \end{aligned} \]
(D17)

(D15)–(D17) in (D12):

\[ \boxed{\;\Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}=\frac{H}{4}\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2+\gamma^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Delta\,\sin2\!\left(\theta_\Sigma-\arctan\frac{\gamma}{\Delta\varepsilon}\right)\;} \]
\[ \boxed{\begin{aligned} \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}&=\frac{H}{4}\left(\Delta\varepsilon^2+\gamma^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Delta\\ &\quad\times\sin2\!\left(\theta_\Sigma-\arctan\frac{\gamma}{\Delta\varepsilon}\right) \end{aligned}} \]
(2)

B. Why $\Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}\gt0$: $K(\theta)$ decreases on 0–45°

Interaction-energy ($G_\mathrm{int}$) model [1]. Each grain has a constant $K_i=K(\theta_i)$; the poster runs use $\theta_1=0$ and $\theta_2=10$–$45^\circ$. The local energy difference is poster Eq. (3):

\[ \Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}=\big[K(\theta_1)-K(\theta_2)\big]\,4\eta_1^2\eta_2^2\,(c-c_0)^2 \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}&=\big[K(\theta_1)-K(\theta_2)\big]\\ &\quad\times4\eta_1^2\eta_2^2\,(c-c_0)^2 \end{aligned} \]
(K1)

The factor $4\eta_1^2\eta_2^2(c-c_0)^2$ is never negative, and it is positive inside the GB once Gd has arrived. So the sign of (K1) is the sign of $K(\theta_1)-K(\theta_2)$, and it only remains to show that $K$ decreases as $|\theta|$ grows from 0 to 45°.

B1. $K(\theta)$

$K(\theta)=Y(\theta)\,\varepsilon_V^2$ with $\varepsilon_V=0.0487$. $Y$ is the coherency modulus of a Gd-rich layer parallel to the GB: the elastic energy per unit $(c-c_0)^2$ of a layer with Vegard eigenstrain $\varepsilon_V\delta_{ij}$, with the stiffness of a grain rotated by $\theta$, that is coherent with the matrix in the GB plane ($\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}_{yy}=\varepsilon^{\mathrm{total}}_{zz}=0$) and free along its normal ($\sigma_{xx}=\sigma_{xy}=0$). The stiffness rotated about [001], with $s\equiv\sin^22\theta$ and $H\equiv C_{11}^f-C_{12}^f-2C_{44}^f$ (as (D3) in part A), is

\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}(\theta)&=C_{11}^f-\tfrac H2 s, & C_{1122}(\theta)&=C_{12}^f+\tfrac H2 s,\\ C_{1212}(\theta)&=C_{44}^f+\tfrac H2 s, & C_{1112}(\theta)&=\tfrac H4\sin 4\theta \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}(\theta)&=C_{11}^f-\tfrac H2 s,\\ C_{1122}(\theta)&=C_{12}^f+\tfrac H2 s,\\ C_{1212}(\theta)&=C_{44}^f+\tfrac H2 s,\\ C_{1112}(\theta)&=\tfrac H4\sin 4\theta \end{aligned} \]
(K2)

In the layer $\varepsilon_{yy}=\varepsilon_{zz}=-\varepsilon_V$ (elastic strain per unit $c-c_0$). The free normal, $\sigma_{xx}=\sigma_{xy}=0$, fixes $\varepsilon_{xx}$ and $\gamma=2\varepsilon_{xy}$:

\[ C_{1111}\,\varepsilon_{xx}+C_{1112}\,\gamma=(C_{1122}+C_{12}^f)\,\varepsilon_V,\qquad C_{1112}\,\varepsilon_{xx}+C_{1212}\,\gamma=-C_{1112}\,\varepsilon_V \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}\,\varepsilon_{xx}+C_{1112}\,\gamma&=(C_{1122}+C_{12}^f)\,\varepsilon_V,\\ C_{1112}\,\varepsilon_{xx}+C_{1212}\,\gamma&=-C_{1112}\,\varepsilon_V \end{aligned} \]
(K3)

The energy density is then $f=\tfrac12(\sigma_{yy}\varepsilon_{yy}+\sigma_{zz}\varepsilon_{zz})=Y\varepsilon_V^2$. With $C_{1112}^2=\tfrac{H^2}{4}s(1-s)$ and $C_{1111}C_{1212}-C_{1112}^2=C_{11}^fC_{44}^f+\tfrac H4(C_{11}^f+C_{12}^f)\,s$, $Y$ depends on $s$ only:

\[ Y(s)=\frac{C_{11}^f+2C_{12}^f}{2}\left[3-\frac{(C_{11}^f+2C_{12}^f)\big(C_{44}^f+\frac H2 s\big)}{C_{11}^fC_{44}^f+\frac H4\big(C_{11}^f+C_{12}^f\big)\,s}\right] \]
\[ \begin{aligned} Y(s)&=\frac{C_{11}^f+2C_{12}^f}{2}\\ &\quad\times\left[3-\frac{(C_{11}^f+2C_{12}^f)\big(C_{44}^f+\frac H2 s\big)}{C_{11}^fC_{44}^f+\frac H4\big(C_{11}^f+C_{12}^f\big)\,s}\right] \end{aligned} \]
(K4)

At $s=0$ this is Cahn's $Y_{100}=(C_{11}+2C_{12})(C_{11}-C_{12})/C_{11}$ [2], and it equals Yeff in the input file.

B2. $K$ decreases on 0–45°, so $\Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}\gt0$

Differentiating (K4),

\[ \frac{dY}{ds}=-\frac{(C_{11}^f+2C_{12}^f)^2\,H\,C_{44}^f\,(C_{11}^f-C_{12}^f)}{8\big[C_{11}^fC_{44}^f+\frac H4(C_{11}^f+C_{12}^f)\,s\big]^2} \]
(K5)

Every factor except $H$ is positive ($C_{44}^f\gt0$, $C_{11}^f\gt C_{12}^f$), so $\operatorname{sign}(dY/ds)=-\operatorname{sign}H$. For this fluorite [3], $H=3288.4-587.8-2\times390.6=1919.4$ eV/nm$^3\gt0$, so $Y$, and with it $K$, falls as $s$ grows. On $0\le|\theta|\le45^\circ$, $s=\sin^22\theta$ grows with $|\theta|$. Therefore

\[ |\theta_1|\lt|\theta_2|\le45^\circ\ \Longrightarrow\ K(\theta_1)\gt K(\theta_2)\ \Longrightarrow\ \Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}\gt0 \]
\[ \begin{gathered} |\theta_1|\lt|\theta_2|\le45^\circ\\ \Longrightarrow\ K(\theta_1)\gt K(\theta_2)\ \Longrightarrow\ \Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}\gt0 \end{gathered} \]
(K6)

In the poster runs $\theta_1=0$, where $K$ is largest (8.695 eV/nm$^3$), so $\Delta G_\mathrm{int}^{1\to2}\gt0$ for every $\theta_2$ = 10–45°: grain 1 has the higher $G_\mathrm{int}$, and grain 2, the one with the larger tilt, grows (Table B1, Fig. B2).

  • $K(-\theta)=K(\theta)$, so the sign of $\theta$ does not matter; $|\theta_1|=|\theta_2|$ gives $\Delta G_\mathrm{int}=0$.
  • $K$ is symmetric about 45°. In general the grain with the larger $\sin^22\theta$ has the smaller $K$.
  • For a cubic material with $H\lt0$ (Zener ratio $2C_{44}/(C_{11}-C_{12})\gt1$) the inequalities in (K6) reverse. Here the ratio is 0.29.
