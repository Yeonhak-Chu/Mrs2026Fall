Phase-Field Simulation of Sintering and DIGM in Gd2O3-ZrO2 Ceramic Composites

Yeonhak Chu, Yanzhou Ji, Young-Jae Kim, Sang-chae Jeon, Kunok Chang

Derivation of Eq. (2)

1. Elastic driving force and stiffness

The elastic driving force (EDF) is the difference in the elastic potential between two grain orientations at the same point $\mathbf r$:

\[ \Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r)=f_\mathrm{el}(\theta_1,\mathbf r)-f_\mathrm{el}(\theta_2,\mathbf r) \]
(A.1)

Because the ZrO2 fluorite phase is cubic, only $C^f_{11}$, $C^f_{12}$, and $C^f_{44}$ are independent:

\[ C_{ijkl}(0^\circ)= \begin{pmatrix} C^f_{11}&C^f_{12}&C^f_{12}&0&0&0\\ C^f_{12}&C^f_{11}&C^f_{12}&0&0&0\\ C^f_{12}&C^f_{12}&C^f_{11}&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0&0\\ 0&0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0\\ 0&0&0&0&0&C^f_{44} \end{pmatrix} \]
\[ \begin{gathered} C_{ijkl}(0^\circ)=\\ \begin{pmatrix} C^f_{11}&C^f_{12}&C^f_{12}&0&0&0\\ C^f_{12}&C^f_{11}&C^f_{12}&0&0&0\\ C^f_{12}&C^f_{12}&C^f_{11}&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0&0\\ 0&0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0\\ 0&0&0&0&0&C^f_{44} \end{pmatrix} \end{gathered} \]
(A.2)

Turning the cube axes by $\theta$ about $[001]$ gives

\[ C_{ijkl}(\theta)= \begin{pmatrix} C_{1111}(\theta)&C_{1122}(\theta)&C^f_{12}&0&0&C_{1112}(\theta)\\ C_{1122}(\theta)&C_{1111}(\theta)&C^f_{12}&0&0&-C_{1112}(\theta)\\ C^f_{12}&C^f_{12}&C^f_{11}&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0&0\\ 0&0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0\\ C_{1112}(\theta)&-C_{1112}(\theta)&0&0&0&C_{1212}(\theta) \end{pmatrix} \]
\[ \begin{gathered} C_{ijkl}(\theta)=\\ \begin{pmatrix} C_{1111}(\theta)&C_{1122}(\theta)&C^f_{12}&0&0&C_{1112}(\theta)\\ C_{1122}(\theta)&C_{1111}(\theta)&C^f_{12}&0&0&-C_{1112}(\theta)\\ C^f_{12}&C^f_{12}&C^f_{11}&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0&0\\ 0&0&0&0&C^f_{44}&0\\ C_{1112}(\theta)&-C_{1112}(\theta)&0&0&0&C_{1212}(\theta) \end{pmatrix} \end{gathered} \]
(A.3)

where

\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}(\theta)&=C^f_{11}-\frac{H}{2}\sin^2 2\theta\\ C_{1122}(\theta)&=C^f_{12}+\frac{H}{2}\sin^2 2\theta\\ C_{1212}(\theta)&=C^f_{44}+\frac{H}{2}\sin^2 2\theta\\ C_{1112}(\theta)&=\frac{H}{4}\sin 4\theta \end{aligned} \qquad H\equiv C^f_{11}-C^f_{12}-2C^f_{44} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} C_{1111}(\theta)&=C^f_{11}-\frac{H}{2}\sin^2 2\theta\\ C_{1122}(\theta)&=C^f_{12}+\frac{H}{2}\sin^2 2\theta\\ C_{1212}(\theta)&=C^f_{44}+\frac{H}{2}\sin^2 2\theta\\ C_{1112}(\theta)&=\frac{H}{4}\sin 4\theta\\ H&\equiv C^f_{11}-C^f_{12}-2C^f_{44} \end{aligned} \]
(A.4)

2. Energy of one grain

$f_\mathrm{el}(\theta,\mathbf r)$ consists of $\theta$-dependent and $\theta$-independent terms. With $\gamma_{xy}\equiv2\varepsilon_{xy}$ and $\Delta\varepsilon\equiv\varepsilon_{xx}-\varepsilon_{yy}$,

\[ f_\mathrm{el}(0^\circ,\mathbf r)=\frac12\Big[C^f_{11}\big(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\big)+2C^f_{12}\,\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}+C^f_{44}\,\gamma_{xy}^2\Big]+(\theta\text{-independent terms}) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} f_\mathrm{el}(0^\circ,\mathbf r)={}&\frac12\Big[C^f_{11}\big(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\big)+2C^f_{12}\,\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}\\ &\quad+C^f_{44}\,\gamma_{xy}^2\Big]+(\theta\text{-independent terms}) \end{aligned} \]
(A.5)
\[ \begin{aligned} f_\mathrm{el}(\theta,\mathbf r) &=\frac12\Big[C_{1111}(\theta)\big(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\big)+2C_{1122}(\theta)\,\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}\\ &\qquad+C_{1212}(\theta)\,\gamma_{xy}^2+2C_{1112}(\theta)\,\gamma_{xy}\big(\varepsilon_{xx}-\varepsilon_{yy}\big)\Big]+(\theta\text{-independent terms})\\ &=f_\mathrm{el}(0^\circ,\mathbf r)-\frac{H}{4}\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2-\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\sin^2 2\theta+\frac{H}{4}\,\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\,\sin 4\theta \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} f_\mathrm{el}(\theta,\mathbf r) ={}&\frac12\Big[C_{1111}(\theta)\big(\varepsilon_{xx}^2+\varepsilon_{yy}^2\big)\\ &\quad+2C_{1122}(\theta)\,\varepsilon_{xx}\varepsilon_{yy}+C_{1212}(\theta)\,\gamma_{xy}^2\\ &\quad+2C_{1112}(\theta)\,\gamma_{xy}\big(\varepsilon_{xx}-\varepsilon_{yy}\big)\Big]\\ &+(\theta\text{-independent terms})\\ ={}&f_\mathrm{el}(0^\circ,\mathbf r)-\frac{H}{4}\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2-\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\sin^2 2\theta\\ &+\frac{H}{4}\,\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\,\sin 4\theta \end{aligned} \]
(A.6)

3. Difference of the two grains

With $\theta_\Delta\equiv\theta_2-\theta_1$ and $\theta_\Sigma\equiv\theta_1+\theta_2$,

\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r) &=f_\mathrm{el}(\theta_1,\mathbf r)-f_\mathrm{el}(\theta_2,\mathbf r)\\ &=-\frac{H}{4}\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2-\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\big(\sin^2 2\theta_1-\sin^2 2\theta_2\big)+\frac{H}{4}\,\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\,\big(\sin 4\theta_1-\sin 4\theta_2\big)\\ &=\frac{H}{4}\sin 2\theta_\Delta\Big[\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2-\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\sin 2\theta_\Sigma-2\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\cos 2\theta_\Sigma\Big] \end{aligned} \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r) ={}&f_\mathrm{el}(\theta_1,\mathbf r)-f_\mathrm{el}(\theta_2,\mathbf r)\\ ={}&-\frac{H}{4}\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2-\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\big(\sin^2 2\theta_1-\sin^2 2\theta_2\big)\\ &+\frac{H}{4}\,\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\,\big(\sin 4\theta_1-\sin 4\theta_2\big)\\ ={}&\frac{H}{4}\sin 2\theta_\Delta\Big[\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2-\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\sin 2\theta_\Sigma\\ &-2\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\cos 2\theta_\Sigma\Big] \end{aligned} \]
(A.7)

Setting $\theta_1=\Phi/2$ and $\theta_2=-\Phi/2$ in Eq. (A.7) gives the expression of Schönfelder et al. [1], $\Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r)=\frac{H}{2}\,\gamma_{xy}\Delta\varepsilon\sin 2\Phi$.

4. Turned frame

If we introduce $\beta\equiv\theta_\Sigma/2-\pi/8$, we get the turned frame:

\[ \begin{gathered} \sin 2\theta_\Sigma=\cos 4\beta,\qquad -\cos 2\theta_\Sigma=\sin 4\beta,\\ \Delta\varepsilon'=\Delta\varepsilon\cos 2\beta+\gamma_{xy}\sin 2\beta,\qquad \gamma_{xy}'=\gamma_{xy}\cos 2\beta-\Delta\varepsilon\sin 2\beta \end{gathered} \]
\[ \begin{gathered} \sin 2\theta_\Sigma=\cos 4\beta,\qquad -\cos 2\theta_\Sigma=\sin 4\beta,\\ \Delta\varepsilon'=\Delta\varepsilon\cos 2\beta+\gamma_{xy}\sin 2\beta,\\ \gamma_{xy}'=\gamma_{xy}\cos 2\beta-\Delta\varepsilon\sin 2\beta \end{gathered} \]
(A.8)

so $\Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r)$ becomes

\[ \Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r)=\frac{H}{4}\sin 2\theta_\Delta\big((\Delta\varepsilon')^2-\gamma_{xy}'^2\big) \]
(A.9)

5. Complex form

Writing the strain in the complex plane gives Fig. 3. Here,

\[ \begin{gathered} 2\alpha=\arctan\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{\Delta\varepsilon},\qquad \psi=2\alpha-2\beta,\\ \zeta\equiv\Delta\varepsilon+i\gamma_{xy}=\rho\,e^{i2\alpha},\qquad \zeta'\equiv\Delta\varepsilon'+i\gamma_{xy}'=\zeta\,e^{-i2\beta}=\rho\,e^{i\psi},\\ (\Delta\varepsilon)^2+\gamma_{xy}^2=(\Delta\varepsilon')^2+\gamma_{xy}'^2=\rho^2 \end{gathered} \]
\[ \begin{gathered} 2\alpha=\arctan\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{\Delta\varepsilon},\qquad \psi=2\alpha-2\beta,\\ \zeta\equiv\Delta\varepsilon+i\gamma_{xy}=\rho\,e^{i2\alpha},\\ \zeta'\equiv\Delta\varepsilon'+i\gamma_{xy}'=\zeta\,e^{-i2\beta}=\rho\,e^{i\psi},\\ (\Delta\varepsilon)^2+\gamma_{xy}^2=(\Delta\varepsilon')^2+\gamma_{xy}'^2=\rho^2 \end{gathered} \]
(A.10)

Therefore,

\[ \Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r)=\frac{H}{4}\sin 2\theta_\Delta\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2+\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\big(\cos^2\psi-\sin^2\psi\big) \]
\[ \begin{aligned} \Delta f_\mathrm{el}^{1\to2}(\mathbf r)={}&\frac{H}{4}\sin 2\theta_\Delta\big((\Delta\varepsilon)^2+\gamma_{xy}^2\big)\\ &\times\big(\cos^2\psi-\sin^2\psi\big) \end{aligned} \]
(A.11)

Inserting Eq. (A.10) into Eq. (A.11) gives Eq. (2):

\[ \boxed{\;\Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}=\frac{H}{4}\left((\Delta\varepsilon)^2+\gamma_{xy}^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Delta\,\sin2\!\left(\theta_\Sigma-\arctan\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{\Delta\varepsilon}\right)\;} \]
\[ \boxed{\begin{aligned} \Delta f_{\mathrm{el}}^{1\to2}&=\frac{H}{4}\left((\Delta\varepsilon)^2+\gamma_{xy}^2\right)\sin2\theta_\Delta\\ &\quad\times\sin2\!\left(\theta_\Sigma-\arctan\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{\Delta\varepsilon}\right) \end{aligned}} \]
(2)
